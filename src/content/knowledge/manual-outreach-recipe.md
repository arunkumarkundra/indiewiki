## Prepare

Define one audience with a recognizable need and one question the outreach should answer. Identify a legitimate source for finding relevant people. Read community rules and do not collect personal data in ways that violate platform terms or reasonable expectations. Draft a short message that states why you are contacting the person and an easy next step.

## Run and observe

Contact a small batch individually. Keep the message truthful and specific; do not imply research participation is unrelated to a sale if it is. Record delivery, replies, opt-outs, conversations held, and whether the person matches your intended audience. Avoid sensitive notes unless necessary.

## Review

Look for objections and mismatches, not just positive replies. A low response could mean poor targeting, weak relevance, bad timing, or an unsuitable channel; it does not prove the problem is absent. Change one variable at a time and stop if recipients signal disinterest. Follow applicable outreach rules and honor opt-outs promptly.
