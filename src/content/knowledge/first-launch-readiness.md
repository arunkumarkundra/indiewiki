## Product and usability

- The core user can complete one meaningful task from start to finish.
- Loading, empty, success, validation, and error states are understandable.
- Keyboard access, visible focus, labels, and mobile layout work on the main journey.
- Product claims match current behavior and limitations are visible.

## Operations and safety

- Authentication and authorization are checked on the server.
- Secrets are outside version control; backups and restore steps are understood.
- Support contact and incident owner are known.
- Analytics events answer a defined question and avoid unnecessary personal data.
- Billing, cancellation, privacy, and terms links work where applicable.

## Release and learning

- Production build succeeds from a clean install.
- Canonical domain, sitemap, robots, social preview, and 404 are checked.
- A rollback or previous known-good release is available.
- First-week feedback route and decision review date are scheduled.

This list cannot prove legal compliance or security. Adapt it to product risk, user expectations, and jurisdiction. Ask a qualified reviewer about high-impact regulated use cases.
