## Start with a reason

A useful first note explains why this specific person is being contacted, what problem or question is relevant to them, and one low-friction next step. Keep the request honest and easy to decline. Do not pretend to have a relationship, imply false urgency, or hide a sales purpose.

The supplied screenshots summarize advice attributed to a YC talk and founder discussions, but do not include enough original context to validate precise formulas or response rates. Treat brevity, targeting, and personalization as practical hypotheses to test, not guarantees.

## A simple structure

> Subject: Question about [specific workflow]
>
> Hi [name] — I noticed [specific, truthful reason for reaching out]. I’m learning how [type of person] handles [concrete task]. Would you be open to a 15-minute conversation next week? No worries if it is not relevant.
>
> [Your name]

Customize the note with genuine context; do not automate personalization that is not true. Send only to an appropriate audience, identify yourself, and honor opt-outs. Rules for commercial email vary by location and recipient; check the requirements that apply before a campaign. Measure useful conversations and product fit, not just opens.
