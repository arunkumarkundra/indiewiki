## Product and research

**Activation:** A user reaches a meaningful early outcome that indicates they understood or received initial value. Define the event for the specific product.

**MVP:** The smallest coherent product experience used to test an important assumption while delivering a real outcome to an intended user.

**Concierge test:** A service or workflow delivered manually behind the scenes to learn what users need before automating it.

**Leading question:** A question that suggests the answer the interviewer expects, weakening the value of the response.

## Engineering and quality

**Canonical URL:** The preferred URL for a page when multiple URLs can show equivalent content.

**INP:** Interaction to Next Paint, a Core Web Vital that measures responsiveness across user interactions.

**Least privilege:** Give a person or software component only the access needed for its task.

**Threat model:** A structured account of assets, trust boundaries, likely threats, and controls for a system.

## Business and growth

**Churn:** Customers or users stop using or paying for a product over a defined period; definitions differ by business model.

**Distribution:** The channels and mechanisms through which intended users discover and adopt a product.

Terms can have specialized meanings in particular industries. Link to a topic page for context rather than treating this glossary as a substitute for domain-specific definitions.
