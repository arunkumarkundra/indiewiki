## Copy and complete

**Decision:** What will we decide after this experiment?

**Uncertainty:** Which assumption could change that decision?

**Audience and context:** Who has the relevant recent experience, and in what situation?

**Method:** What will we show, ask, or observe? How will we recruit without misleading people?

**Evidence to record:** What behavior, commitment, or repeated pattern would count? What result would count against the idea?

**Limits:** What can this sample or method not tell us?

**Time and cost:** When will we stop, and what resources are needed?

**Decision rule:** Given the likely outcomes, what will we do next?

**Result:** Record observations separately from interpretation. Include surprises and counterexamples.

**Follow-up:** Keep, change, or stop the hypothesis? Who owns the next step and by when?

## Use it honestly

A small experiment reduces a named uncertainty; it rarely proves an entire market or business. Set decision rules before seeing results where possible, so enthusiasm does not shift the goalposts. Store personal information only when needed and use anonymized notes for shared project records.
