## Understand the economics

Estimate recurring costs, payment processing, support time, refunds, taxes, and the work required to deliver each sale. Track cash commitments and renewal dates. A profitable-looking price can still be unsustainable if delivery is labor intensive or acquisition costs exceed contribution.

## Set clear customer expectations

Explain what is sold, who provides it, when access begins, how cancellation and refunds work, and how customers can get help. Keep records sufficient for bookkeeping and support, but do not collect unnecessary identity or payment details. Use a reputable payment provider for card data rather than handling it yourself.

## Get local advice when needed

Business registration, tax, privacy, consumer protection, accessibility, and sales rules vary by jurisdiction and business model. The wiki cannot infer your obligations from a generic checklist. Consult current official local guidance or a qualified professional before making a consequential decision. Recheck obligations as your customer locations, team, and revenue model change.
