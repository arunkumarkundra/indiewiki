IndieWiki is a free, open field manual for people who build products on their own or in small teams: developers and non-developers, first-timers and people on their tenth launch. It collects the practical knowledge that is usually scattered across threads, screenshots and bookmarks, checks it, and keeps it current.

It is published by [Prisha iCube](https://pi3.in/) and edited by its community. The text is shared under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence, and the code that builds the site is open source.

## What we promise readers

- **Practical over impressive.** Every page should help you do something today.
- **Checked against the source.** Prices, limits and rules are checked on the provider's own pages, and every tool shows the month it was last checked.
- **Dated.** Every page shows when an editor last reviewed it.
- **Plain words.** Short sentences, common words, terms explained. No hype.
- **Honest about trade-offs.** Every tool lists its weak points, and pages say when we are not sure.

## How we choose tools

A tool appears in the [Toolbox](/tools/) when:

1. Indie founders use it for real work, and it solves a problem covered in the wiki.
2. Its pricing, free plan and key limits can be confirmed on the vendor's own website or documentation.
3. It is actively maintained.

"**Start here**" marks a sensible default for most indie founders in that category. It is a starting point, not a ranking, and the right choice depends on your situation.

"**Checked**" with a month means an editor compared the entry's pricing and free-plan details with the vendor's own pages that month. Prices change often; always confirm on the vendor's pricing page before you rely on a number.

## No ads, no affiliate links, no paid placements

IndieWiki does not sell listings, does not use affiliate links, and does not run ads or trackers. Vendors are welcome to correct facts about their own products through the normal [contribution process](/contribute/), and must say who they are when they do.

## How pages are written

The first version of this wiki was researched and drafted with help from an AI assistant, with every price, limit and policy checked against official sources and reviewed by the maintainers before publishing. New contributions may also use AI tools. Whatever the tool, a person is responsible for each change, and every change is reviewed before it is published. We cut filler, check claims, and prefer real examples.

## Corrections

If something is wrong or out of date, please [report a problem](/contribute/). We fix factual errors quickly and note the new review date on the page.

## Not professional advice

Pages about law, tax and finance explain common situations so you know what to ask. They are not legal, tax or financial advice. For decisions with real consequences, talk to a qualified professional in your country.
