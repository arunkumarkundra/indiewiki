**In short:** we do not collect personal data from readers. There are no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts on this site. The few things your browser remembers stay in your browser.

This policy covers the website at indie.pi3.in, published by Prisha iCube.

## When you read the site

- **No cookies and no tracking.** We do not set cookies, use analytics, run ads, or load scripts, fonts or images from other companies. Fonts and search run from this site only.
- **Hosting logs.** The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the service. We do not receive or use those logs. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.
- **Links to other sites.** Pages link to tools and sources on other websites. Once you follow a link, that site's privacy policy applies.

## What your browser stores

The site saves a few preferences in your browser's local storage so they survive a page reload:

| What | Why | Where it goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your light or dark theme choice | So the site looks the way you chose | Stays in your browser |
| Ticks on checklists | So you can pick up where you left off | Stays in your browser |

This data is never sent to us or anyone else. You can clear it at any time with the **Reset** button on a checklist, or by clearing site data in your browser settings.

## When you search

Search runs entirely in your browser. The first time you search, your browser downloads the site's search index, a single file from this site, and matches your words locally. Your search terms are not sent anywhere.

## When you contribute

Contributions happen on GitHub. If you report a problem, suggest a tool or edit a page, GitHub processes your account information under its own privacy statement, and your GitHub username and contribution are public on the repository, as they are for any open-source project.

## When you email us

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply and to improve the wiki, and we keep it only as long as needed for that. We do not add you to any mailing list.

## Your rights

You can ask what personal data we hold about you (for most readers, none), and ask us to correct or delete it, under laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act and the EU's GDPR. Contact us using the details on the [contact page](/contact/).

## Children

The site is a general reference for founders and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its review date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the project's repository.
