IndieWiki is a field manual for people building a product on their own or with a small team. It is organised as a **route**: eight stages in the order most products meet them. You do not have to read it in order. Find the stage you are in, do what it says, and come back when you get stuck.

Every page shows the date an editor last reviewed it. Every tool shows the month its prices were checked. If something is out of date, [tell us](/contribute/).

## The route in one screen

| Stage | You are done when |
| --- | --- |
| [1. Start](/start/) | Ten people who have the problem have told you how they deal with it today, and a few want to try your fix. |
| [2. Set up](/setup/) | You own a domain, can send and receive email on it, and every account is in a password manager with two-step login. |
| [3. Build](/build/) | A first version works for one real user, on a stack you understand well enough to fix at 2 a.m. |
| [4. Ship](/ship/) | The site is indexed, has a privacy policy, passes the security and accessibility checks, and the basics (favicon, sharing preview, 404 page) are in place. |
| [5. Launch](/launch/) | You have posted in the places your users read, answered every comment, and collected sign-ups. |
| [6. Grow](/grow/) | You have one repeatable way of finding new users each week. |
| [7. Earn](/earn/) | Someone you do not know has paid you. |
| [8. Run](/run/) | You hear about outages and errors before customers do, backups restore, and the monthly bill holds no surprises. |

## If you do not write code

You can build a real product today with AI app builders and coding agents. The risk is not that you cannot get something working; it is that you ship something you cannot see inside. Three habits keep you safe:

1. **Own the code.** Make sure your project lives in a GitHub repository in your name, not only inside a builder's website. Most builders can sync to GitHub.
2. **Work in small, checked steps.** Ask for one change at a time, test it, and save a version before the next. [Building with AI coding tools](/start/building-with-ai/) describes a workflow that works.
3. **Use the checklists as your contract.** Paste the [security checklist](/ship/security-checklist/) and the [SEO checklist](/ship/seo-checklist/) into your AI tool and ask it to go through them one item at a time, explaining each change. Then verify with the free scanners we list.

Read next: [Validate your idea](/start/validate-your-idea/), [Choose a stack](/build/choose-your-stack/), [Accounts and ownership](/setup/accounts-and-ownership/).

## If you do write code

You will move fast through Build. The places technical founders most often lose weeks are elsewhere:

- Building for months before talking to anyone. Do [Validate your idea](/start/validate-your-idea/) first, even if it feels slow.
- Picking a free host or plan that forbids commercial use. Check [Hosting your website](/build/hosting/).
- Skipping the boring parts that block launch: [privacy policy and terms](/ship/privacy-and-legal/), [app store review](/launch/app-store-submission/), [tax on payments](/earn/accepting-payments/).
- No outreach plan. Read [The first 100 users](/grow/first-100-users/) before launch day, not after.

## The minimum setup for a real product

This is the smallest set of things that lets a stranger find, trust and pay for your product. Everything links to how-to pages.

- A domain you own, at a registrar with fair renewals: [Choose and register a domain](/setup/choose-a-domain/)
- Email on that domain that can send replies: [Email on your custom domain](/setup/custom-domain-email/)
- Hosting whose rules allow selling: [Hosting your website](/build/hosting/)
- A privacy policy and terms that match what the product really does: [Privacy, terms and the law](/ship/privacy-and-legal/)
- Google Search Console set up and a sitemap submitted: [Google Search Console](/ship/google-search-console/)
- A way to take money with tax handled: [Accepting payments](/earn/accepting-payments/)
- An uptime alarm and error tracking: [Monitoring and backups](/run/monitoring-and-backups/)
