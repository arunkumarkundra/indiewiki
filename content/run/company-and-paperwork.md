::: note Not legal or tax advice
Company and tax rules depend on your country and situation. This page explains the usual path so you know what to ask. Talk to an accountant before choosing a structure.
:::

## When to register a company

Many founders start as individuals and register when one of these happens:

- Revenue becomes steady and the tax or liability benefits of a company outweigh the cost of running one.
- A payment provider, marketplace or enterprise customer requires it.
- You take investment or bring in a co-founder with shares.
- The product carries real liability (finance, health, physical goods).

## Common starting structures

| Where | Common choice for small software businesses | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| India | Sole proprietorship, LLP, or Private Limited Company | Private Limited is expected by investors; LLP has lighter compliance; incorporation is done online through the Ministry of Corporate Affairs |
| United States | LLC, or a Delaware C-Corporation for venture funding | Services such as Stripe Atlas and Clerky handle formation for non-US founders |
| United Kingdom | Private limited company | Registered with Companies House online |

## Separate the money

- Open a business bank account (or a separate account even as an individual) and run every business payment through it.
- Use one card for all business subscriptions so the monthly review is easy. See [Cost control](/run/cost-control/).
- Keep every invoice and receipt, as PDFs in one folder per month.
- Pay yourself on a schedule rather than dipping into the business account.

## Records to keep from day one

- [ ] Invoices and receipts for every expense, including software subscriptions billed in foreign currency.
- [ ] Payout reports from your payment provider or merchant of record.
- [ ] Contracts with freelancers, including who owns the work they create.
- [ ] Domain, trademark and company registration documents.
- [ ] A simple monthly summary: revenue, costs, cash in the bank.

## Own your intellectual property

Make sure everything your product depends on belongs to you or your company: the code (including work by freelancers, assigned in writing), the domain, the brand, and the accounts. See [Accounts and ownership](/setup/accounts-and-ownership/).

::: india If you are in India
Ask your chartered accountant about GST registration thresholds, how export of services is treated, and how payouts from foreign merchants of record are reported. Also ask about professional tax and the deadlines for advance tax. Setting this up correctly in the first year is far cheaper than fixing it later.
:::
