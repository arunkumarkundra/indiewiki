# Governance

This lightweight document describes how IndieWiki is maintained and how decisions are made.

## Roles

- **Project owner:** holds repository administration and appoints/removes maintainers. Currently this is the repository owner unless the project records another arrangement.
- **Maintainers:** review contributions, steward editorial quality and repository health, resolve routine disputes, and communicate decisions.
- **Contributors:** anyone proposing, reviewing, correcting, or discussing work.

No additional maintainers or succession process have been named.

## Decisions and editorial authority

Prefer open discussion in issues and pull requests, recording consequential decisions for future contributors. Maintainers handle routine editorial matters against the project principles and policies. Significant changes to purpose, contribution terms, governance, or knowledge map should receive notice and an opportunity for feedback. When views differ, clarify the question, evidence, and affected audience; record the rationale for consequential choices.

The project owner retains final authority over repository administration and unresolved project-level decisions. This is not a voting or consensus guarantee.

A merged page is an editorial contribution, not endorsement of every source or a promise of universal applicability. Maintainers may request revisions, mark a page for review, or remove material that is unsafe, private, materially misleading, out of scope, or inadequately sourced. Seek another qualified review for disputed/high-impact material when available.

## Corrections, conflicts, and moderation

Anyone may report a problem. Prioritize exposed secrets/personal data, dangerous instructions, legal/security inaccuracies, and materially outdated claims. Remove sensitive content promptly and consider whether repository history or credentials need remediation; document the correction without repeating the information.

Disclose relevant financial, employment, or vendor relationships when recommending products. Prioritize reader usefulness and evidence; record meaningful conflicts and their handling.

Keep discussions respectful and focused. Maintainers may remove spam, harassment, discriminatory content, personal information, or repeated disruption, explaining decisions when safe and practical. A fuller code of conduct and appeal path may be adopted as the community grows.

## Changing this document

Propose governance changes through an issue or pull request, explaining rationale and likely effects. Maintainers decide after reasonable discussion; record the outcome in Git history and summarize it in the pull request.

## Current gaps

The project has not defined a formal license, code of conduct, maintainer roster beyond the owner, succession plan, voting rules, appeal process, review service levels, or automated enforcement. Adopt these deliberately rather than implying they exist.
