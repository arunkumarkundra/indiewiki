# Code of Conduct

IndieWiki is built by people at every level of experience, from first-time founders to veterans. We want every one of them to feel welcome to ask, correct and contribute.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners. Every expert was new once.
- Critique the content, not the person. Explain why something is wrong and how to fix it.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Disclose any connection to a company or product you discuss.
- Respect people's time: keep discussions on topic.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Spam, undisclosed promotion, or pushing your own product into unrelated pages.
- Copying other people's work without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently block people who repeatedly or seriously break it. To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately using the details on <https://indiewiki.pi3.in/contact/>. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any space run by the project.
